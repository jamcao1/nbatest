BEHIND THE NUMBERS

A model you can inspect.

Recent results shape team offense and defense, adjusted for opponents and home court. A rest adjustment and correlated score variation turn those estimates into 1,000 possible games.

Model assumptions & limitations

    Scores are not possession-based efficiency ratings. Injuries, rotations, travel, and roster changes are not included in this first model. Odds never determine the simulated scores.
