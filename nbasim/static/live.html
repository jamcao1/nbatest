How the estimate works

Estimated possessions use field-goal attempts + 0.44 × free-throw attempts − individual offensive rebounds + total turnovers, averaged across both teams. This is a box-score approximation, not official tracking pace. Early-quarter estimates are especially noisy.

The model blends observed pace with twelve minutes of historical pace and scoring with thirty possessions of prior efficiency. Team/opponent priors are shrunk toward the league. It simulates remaining scores with shared pace uncertainty and resolves full-game ties through simulated overtime. The displayed 80% range is a model range, not a guarantee.

Late-game fouling, tactical changes, lineup substitutions, injuries, and shot quality are not modeled. Recent scoring does not have to “catch up.” Data age, short samples, large leads, old market prices, and stale team priors suppress watch signals. An experimental watch is a model/price difference, not a recommendation to bet.
